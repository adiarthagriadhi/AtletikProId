.env / Environment Variables
----------------------------
Jangan timpa JWT_SECRET / Midtrans yang sudah jalan.
SMTP (opsional):
  APP_PUBLIC_URL=https://atletikpro.id
  SMTP_HOST=smtp.hostinger.com
  SMTP_PORT=465
  SMTP_SECURE=true
  SMTP_USER=admin@atletikpro.id
  SMTP_PASS=...
  SMTP_FROM=Atletik Pro Id <admin@atletikpro.id>
  HIDE_RESET_LINK=true
  NODE_ENV=production

  ANNUAL_PRICE_IDR=299000
